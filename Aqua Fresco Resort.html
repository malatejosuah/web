Our Story

A sanctuary
born from water

Aqua Fresco Resort was built with one vision — to give every guest a place to breathe, rest, and reconnect with nature. Surrounded by lush Bicolano landscapes in Goa, Camarines Sur, our resort offers crystalline pools, open-air spaces, and warm Filipino hospitality.

Whether you're celebrating a milestone, unwinding with family, or simply escaping the city, Aqua Fresco is your home away from home.
